What we collect

The public submission form may collect:

  • Your name, and an email address or phone number so we can reach you
  • A return mailing address when you ship cards in
  • Whether you plan to ship cards or drop them off in person at a show or Houston meetup
  • Card details (including set, year, number or variant), condition notes, declared value, the service you want, a referral code, and any other notes you add
  • Photos you upload of the cards (front, back and optional close-ups)
  • Your agreement to the terms shown on the form

The Track Your Submission tool uses the reference number we give you. Confirmation and status emails, when you leave an email address, are sent from phcollectibleshtx@gmail.com.

How that information is handled

  • Submissions are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored so the business can quote, schedule and update your order (a Google Sheet [to confirm] and related email).
  • Photos are uploaded to Cloudinary, then stored with the submission.
  • The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.
  • This site does not currently include a visitor-analytics or advertising pixel. If that changes, this page should be updated.
  • Quoted totals are confirmed after inspection. Payment details are arranged with you directly; this site does not itself process card payments.
